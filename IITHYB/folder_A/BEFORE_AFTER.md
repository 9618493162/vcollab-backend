## 🎨 Design Principles Applied

### Google Meet Style Guidelines:
1. **Minimalism**: Clean, uncluttered interface
2. **Hierarchy**: Clear information priority
3. **Consistency**: Uniform spacing and sizing
4. **Feedback**: Hover states and active indicators
5. **Accessibility**: High contrast, readable fonts
6. **Professional**: Corporate-ready appearance
7. **Intuitive**: Common patterns users know
8. **Responsive**: Works on all screen sizes
