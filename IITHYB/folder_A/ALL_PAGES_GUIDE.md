## 📱 How to Navigate

### **From Dashboard:**
- Click **"Start Meeting"** → create-meeting.html
- Click **"Join Meeting"** → join-meeting.html
- Click **"My Meetings"** card → meetings-list.html
- Click **"Chat"** card → chat.html
- Click **"Profile"** card → profile.html

### **From Meeting Room:**
- Click **📞 Leave** → Returns to dashboard
- Click **💬** → Opens chat sidebar
- Click **⋮ More** → Shows all features

### **From Any Page:**
- Click **"Dashboard"** in navbar → dashboard.html
- Click **"Meetings"** in navbar → meetings-list.html
- Click **"Chat"** in navbar → chat.html
- Click **"Profile"** in navbar → profile.html
- Click **"Logout"** → Returns to index.html
