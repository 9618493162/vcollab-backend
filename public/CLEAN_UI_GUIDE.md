
### **Main Controls (6 buttons):**

1. **🎤 Microphone** 
   - White when ON
   - Gray when OFF (muted)
   - Click to toggle

2. **📹 Camera**
   - White when ON
   - Gray when OFF
   - Click to toggle

3. **😊 Reactions**
   - Opens emoji picker
   - 6 animated reactions
   - Click emoji to send

4. **🖥️ Present**
   - Share your screen
   - Share window
   - Share tab

5. **⋮ More** (NEW!)
   - Opens dropdown menu
   - All other features here
   - Clean organization

6. **📞 Leave**
   - Red button
   - End call
   - Confirmation dialog

## 💡 How to Use

### **Basic Controls** (Always visible):
1. Click 🎤 to mute/unmute
2. Click 📹 to camera on/off
3. Click 😊 for reactions
4. Click 🖥️ to share screen
5. Click 📞 to leave

### **Advanced Features** (In More menu):
1. Click **⋮ More**
2. Menu pops up
3. Click any option
4. Menu closes automatically

### **Quick Access:**
- **Ctrl + D** - Toggle mic
- **Ctrl + E** - Toggle camera
- **F11** - Fullscreen
